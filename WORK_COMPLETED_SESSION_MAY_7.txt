I. PROBLEM SOLVED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE ISSUE:
  • System hitting Binance 1200 req/min rate limit after 2 minutes
  • 10-minute IP ban (418 HTTP error) triggered by aggressive polling
  • Ban timestamps persisting across restarts → infinite ban loop
  • System could NOT trade at all

ROOT CAUSE:
  • Balance sync polling every 5 seconds (240 req/min)
  • Orders polling every 5 seconds (240 req/min)
  • Market data polling every 2 seconds (120 req/min)
  • Total: 600 req/min (aggressive)
  • Binance limit: 1200 req/min (hit in 2 minutes)
