## Daily-cause attribution: why is the observed outcome ~0 trades/day?

Ranked by evidential strength (strongest/most directly observed first):

1. **Strategy cause — STRONGEST, directly evidenced.** The `ConfFloor=0.95` requirement that blocked the one candidate this session is not an arbitrary tuning choice; the same session's own log shows the floor exists *because* confidence buckets 0.65-0.85 measure 0-12.2% historical win rate (`runtime_timeline.md` T+42s), which is fully consistent with the separately-proven, much larger-sample finding (3,140 backtest samples + 69 live trades, both 5m and 1h) that the ML forecaster's confidence score has **no predictive relationship with net P&L** (`edge-verdict-no-edge.md`). In other words: the system is deliberately gating almost everything out because the underlying signal has no demonstrated edge, and 0.95 is roughly where the model's own historical calibration stops being actively harmful. Raising the floor would not fix this — the confidence score itself doesn't rank-order outcomes. This is a **strategy/signal problem**, not a filter-tuning problem, and it is the dominant, best-evidenced cause of zero trades.
2. **Wiring/timing cause — SECOND STRONGEST, directly observed, but likely secondary in daily impact.** `PERSIST_GATE` requires 2 consecutive confirming 5-minute bars; the observation window (~54s of active cycling) was shorter than one bar, so the streak mechanically could never advance to 2 regardless of signal quality. This is a real contributor to the *specific 90-second sample* showing 0 trades, but it does not generalize to "0 trades/day" — over a real trading day there are ~288 five-minute bars, so PERSIST_GATE would advance many times if ConfFloor were passable. This cause explains the sample, not the steady-state daily outcome; cause #1 explains the steady state.
3. **Data cause — MODERATE, observed, likely a minor multiplier not a root cause.** Symbol discovery returned 0 symbols on first scan and took a full polling cycle to populate (`runtime_timeline.md` T+36s, T+48s), forcing a fallback to 10 hardcoded `DEFAULT_SYMBOLS` for at least one cycle and causing WebSocket universe churn (2 reconnects in 54s). This could suppress signal generation on the "real" discovered symbols during the cold-start window, but has no evidence of persisting after warm-up, and would not on its own explain a sustained zero-trades outcome across a full day once discovery settles.
4. **Risk, capital, execution, and exit causes — NOT EVIDENCED as contributing, because none of these stages were ever reached.** Stages 5-13 (arbitration risk gates, affordability/`gate_5_capital`, order submission, entry fills, TP/SL management, exit fills, net-profitability, compounding) recorded **0 observed, never exercised** — there is no evidence they are broken, and no evidence they work; they simply never had a candidate to act on. Any claim that risk sizing, capital allocation, execution latency, or exit logic is "the" cause of zero trades would be speculation not supported by this session's data. If ConfFloor/PERSIST_GATE were ever cleared, these stages would need their own dedicated observation before they could be ruled in or out.
5. **Market cause — WEAKEST, plausible but unfalsifiable from one 54-second sample.** The session logged `market_regime=DOWNTREND portfolio_state=CASH_HEAVY allowed=False` (`runtime_timeline.md` T+43s) and Fear & Greed = 28 ("Fear") at session start. A persistently bearish/fearful regime could independently suppress BUY-side candidates via the regime gate (`arbitration_engine.py` `NO_AVGDOWN_REGIMES` / per-symbol downtrend veto), but since arbitration was never invoked this session (stage 5), this gate's actual effect could not be observed directly — the regime classification is confirmed, but its downstream gating effect on trade count is inferred, not measured.

### Bottom line

The dominant, best-evidenced cause is **strategy** (no demonstrated predictive edge in the live ML confidence signal, with the system's own 0.95 confidence floor functioning as an explicit, log-visible admission of that fact). The **wiring/timing** (PERSIST_GATE vs. short observation window) and **data** (cold-start discovery) causes are real but explain this specific short sample more than they explain a sustained daily zero-trade outcome. **Risk, capital, execution, and exit** causes cannot presently be ruled in or out — they were never reached by any candidate in either observation session, and treating them as "clean" would overstate what has actually been verified.
