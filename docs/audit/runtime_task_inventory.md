## Priority follow-up from this inventory

1. **Orphaned training task on shutdown** — the most concrete, runtime-confirmed defect in this pass. `supervisor.sh`'s stall watchdog force-restarts the process if no "cycle N" log line appears for 180s; a hung/slow training task extending shutdown by 80+ seconds is within that tolerance today, but is a latent risk (a slower training run, or one that hangs, could trigger a forced kill mid-training, or delay a restart after a crash).
2. **`_phase_recover()` / 300s TP/SL aged-position recalculation** — confirmed to live only on `NativeOrchestrator.run_loop()`, which is not the actual production cycle driver. Needs a source-level check (not just runtime observation, since 142s wasn't long enough to see a 300s tick either way) of whether `main.py::trading_cycle()` independently invokes this logic or whether it's effectively dead in production.
