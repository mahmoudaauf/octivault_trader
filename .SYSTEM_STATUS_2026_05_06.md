## Known Limitations & Mitigations

### 1. Polling Latency (5-10 seconds)
- **Limitation**: Fill detected 5-10s after execution
- **Acceptable for**: Swing trading (minute+ timeframes)
- **Not suitable for**: Scalping (need WebSocket)
- **Mitigation**: WebSocket integration on roadmap

### 2. Bulk Limit (last 10 trades per symbol)
- **Limitation**: Only fetches 10 most recent trades
- **Risk**: High-frequency traders might miss fills
- **Acceptable if**: <10 fills per 5s per symbol (typical)
- **Mitigation**: Reconciliation logic on roadmap

### 3. No Partial Fill Coalescence
- **Limitation**: Multi-leg orders appear as multiple positions initially
- **Behavior**: Averaging is automatic but timing may feel off
- **Mitigation**: By design (correct approach for position tracking)
